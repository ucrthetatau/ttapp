Add TT Hub to your iPhone

Takes 1 minute. Needed for notifications.

thetatauucr.vercel.app
1

Open it in Safari

Use the blue compass app. Links inside Discord, Instagram or Chrome won't work, so copy the link into Safari.

2

Tap the Share button

A square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen. If you don't see it, tap the three dots (•••) first.

3

Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down in the menu that pops up until you see it.

4

Leave "Open as Web App" on, tap Add

The button is in the top right corner.

5

Open TT Hub from your home screen

Look for the new icon. Sign in with your Google account.

6

Allow notifications

Tap "Allow notifications" when the pop-up shows, then Allow. You'll get votes, announcements and dues reminders.

That's it. You're in.
