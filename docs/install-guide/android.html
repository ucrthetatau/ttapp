Add TT Hub to your Android

Takes 1 minute. Needed for the best experience.

thetatauucr.vercel.app
1

Open it in Chrome

Links inside Discord or Instagram open in a small browser that can't install apps, so copy the link into Chrome.

2

Tap the three dots

In the top right corner of Chrome.

3

Tap "Add to Home screen"

Some phones call it "Install app". Either one is right.

4

Tap Install (or Add)

Confirm in the box that pops up. If it asks again, tap Add.

5

Open TT Hub from your home screen

Look for the new icon. Sign in with your Google account.

6

Allow notifications

Tap "Allow notifications" when the pop-up shows, then Allow. You'll get votes, announcements and dues reminders.

That's it. You're in.
